# IMPORTANT — Whole-Object preservation, AI collection access and partnership opportunity

Date: 2026-10-20 (owner's local date).
Priority: **IMPORTANT strategic owner direction.**
Status: future product/integration and business proposal, not a shipped capability, approved data program, external agreement or established OpenAI partnership.

## Enduring purpose

**Preserve the whole Object from the beginning; create once, transpose and reuse.** This extends Atlas's current transposable-Object foundation rather than replacing it with an AI product.

The owner's motivating question is how much of the earlier internet has been lost, including its context and relationships. No historical loss percentage is asserted here. Atlas should help prevent future loss by preserving intelligible, reusable content and its relationships while people collect and create it, rather than trying to reconstruct everything after sources disappear.

## Whole-Object preservation

For supported and permitted captures, preserve original content and asset bytes, source URLs, authorship/attribution where known, capture times, hierarchy, placements, typed relationships, citations, versions and presentation intent. Preserve which image illustrates which passage, which source supports which statement and how a collection became an article.

Distinguish original source material, user annotations, inferred relations and generated material. Unknown provenance remains unknown; inferred links must not masquerade as source facts.

A link or thumbnail is not a complete archive. Record capture scope, dependencies, completeness, externally referenced/missing assets and failed captures. Do not claim arbitrary websites are fully preserved or bypass access restrictions. Keep durable local/optional remote storage and tested restoration separate from browser caches.

Machine-readable manifests and versioned schemas should preserve meaningful relationships independently of a particular UI or AI vendor. This could benefit human readers, restoration tools, retrieval systems and eligible future training datasets; usefulness and quality require evaluation rather than assumption.

## Permission-based AI understanding of collections

Owner ambition: an eventual ChatGPT/OpenAI integration or partnership in which ChatGPT can see and understand the user's explicitly shared Atlas collections, including Objects, notes, images, sources, hierarchy and relationships—not merely a flattened dump of unrelated files.

Example: a student selects a research Collection and asks, “Use this collection to help me write my paper.” An authorized integration supplies the selected supported content and its relationship structure, allowing assistance grounded in the user's organized material.

Proposed integration obligations:
- User selects the Collection/Object scope and can inspect what is being shared, the destination and the purpose.
- Private libraries remain private by default. Sharing a view or subset does not silently expose unrelated parents, linked private Objects, credentials or the entire library.
- Preserve stable references, source attribution, versions and relationship types in the handoff. Declare unavailable content and bound context/asset sizes.
- Read access is distinct from permission to modify, move, delete or publish. Any future write-back uses separately authorized, version-aware Atlas operations.
- Revoke future access and explain the limits of deleting material already transferred under a partner's applicable terms. Do not promise recall of already processed data.
- Treat collected webpages and documents as untrusted content, not authority to execute instructions or expand access.
- Keep the same portable contract usable by other authorized tools and AI providers. A commercial partnership must not make the user's library dependent on one partner.

This document does not select an API, authentication implementation, connector protocol or specific OpenAI product entitlement.

## Optional structured contribution and training

A separate owner idea is for willing users to contribute selected structured material and relationship-rich datasets to an AI company. This may offer better context than disconnected examples, but no training benefit or partner demand is established.

**Collection access for personal assistance is separate from permission to contribute material for training.** Purchasing Atlas, using a partner integration, or sharing a Collection for a question must not silently enroll the user in training-data contribution.

Any future contribution program needs explicit, understandable opt-in; selected scope and purpose; applicable content rights and third-party privacy review; provenance and consent records; clear retention and withdrawal/deletion terms; and separate design for any student/minor participation under applicable requirements. Possession of a file is not itself permission to license it for training. Do not transfer private collections or activate contribution on the basis of this proposal.

## Proposed distribution economics

Owner proposal: potentially raise the Atlas price from $1 to $2 and give an AI/distribution partner half—illustratively $1 to the partner and $1 gross retained by Atlas per $2 transaction before fees, taxes, refunds and operating costs.

The owner would accept this revenue share in exchange for meaningful partner distribution, marketing reach and credibility. This is marketing funded through revenue sharing, not a proven zero-cost acquisition channel.

Payment cadence, attribution rules, distribution commitments, costs, net proceeds and negotiated terms remain undecided here. This record does not change live pricing or establish a subscription.

Keep distribution/revenue sharing, permission-based collection assistance and optional dataset contribution as distinct agreements and user choices. Access to private user data is not automatic compensation for distribution.

No company has been contacted, no agreement is in place, no sharing has been enabled and no customer data has been sent by this documentation task. Do not represent the proposal as OpenAI endorsement, guaranteed distribution or approved training use.

## Proposed acceptance checks

Verify exact-scope export including private cross-link exclusion; faithful identity/provenance/relationship transfer; explicit missing-resource declarations; authorization and revocation; no write escalation through read sharing; independent training-consent controls; original-content integrity; and useful operation/export without a partner account.

Assess dataset completeness, provenance, duplication, rights and downstream usefulness independently before claiming training readiness. Partnership conversion, distribution reach and unit economics require actual evidence.
