## Periods and versions
Use actual dates as period anchors, not arbitrary numbered eras. `periodDate` is when the idea entered the record, `revision` and `packageVersion` identify which statement of the idea, and staging/production evidence dates are different events. Future amendments create separate new date-stamped packages with explicit `amends`/`supersedes`; do not erase the older version.

## Canonical manifest gate
Existing `forever/manifest.json` **can** evolve, but **only after implementation demonstrates staging and production-value acceptance**. Append accepted references to its appropriate arrays, preserving all existing entries. Link proof; promote verified subcapabilities rather than the whole aspirational package. Draft records here are intentionally not in that manifest. These records follow examples of Forever Works 0.1 but have **not yet been independently validated** against upstream normative schemas.
