# Why / What / Who / When / Where / How — Atlas living knowledge
**Owner-directed period 2026-10-15 · Revision 1 · Not implemented**

## WHY
Traditional bookmarks are frequently forgotten because they capture WHERE but not WHY. Atlas is foremost an exercise in fun: elegant Pinterest/Instagram-like saving, labeling, collecting and rediscovering. By adding optional text and tags to every collected thing, a person organically creates years of structured, navigable, enduring self-authored data. That data may enrich a future authorized agent's understanding but not permit claims of reading minds or unwarranted psychological certainty.

## WHAT
**Catalog experience:** Create a catalog in the catalog/relationships tab, pin its reorderable icon to a scrollable left sidebar and open a smooth, accessible, beautiful presentation of directories, bookmarks, images, galleries, files, videos, tags and connections. Same canonical data, multiple masonry, cards, editorial, compact, directory or graph layouts.

**Capture:** Import browser bookmarks; Save with minimal required interaction; optionally add image from local or remote source, title, description, note about what it reminds the owner of, tags and linked references. Every object and even the *relationship* may have a distinct text field, title, tags and dated notes. A relationship may preserve “I paired this image with this bookmark because ...”. Neither the notes nor tags are required chores.

**Mixed media:** Gallery can contain annotated images, each separately linked to documents, websites, video or other files. A bookmark can collect media references of several types. Draw accurate, actionable filtered counters such as “3 Documents · 2 Connections · 1 Video · 5 Files”. Share logical attachment identities rather than duplicating physical bytes. Preserve source, provenance and availability.

**Graphmarks-inspired maps:** Graph circles represent sites, URLs, catalogs, notes, tags, media, files and R2 resources. User can click a circle to open a bookmark or preview detail in a right sidebar, show explanatory edges, select neighbors, zoom or filter clusters. Circle size can express visits, optionally observed foreground active dwell time, adjustable normalized blend or uniform; time ranges and uncertain measurement are transparent. An open background tab is not evidence of active reading.

**Unified inspector:** In the extension full page, retain a live graph left and a right inspector, optionally split horizontally: selected authorized local resource **top**, selected authorized R2 resource **bottom**. Pin, swap, resize and open. R2 previews should feel as immediate as local previews where provider and network allow, while always respecting permissions, network transfer and signed URL lifetimes. No secret exposure.

**Publication:** A user who has organized images, descriptions, tags and links has already done the editorial work. Provide Create Article / Gallery Site from selected sources. Let users supply an explicit Title independent of source filename. Select which links are included; place them below meaningful text or as citations. Reuse notes/captions as editable sections; create independently titled articles, photo essays, portfolios and research galleries. Editing the publication never silently rewrites the original notes; source references may be pinned snapshots or explicitly refreshed links. Generate polished responsive static sites and future timelines, charts and graph visualizations from the same JSON.

**Portable AKF:** A documented versioned JSON export includes stable object IDs, typed links, why-linked explanations, independent annotations, tags, timestamps, provenance, file manifests, inclusion vs remote reference, optional visual layout and privacy selections. Programmatic users can consume/rebuild it independently; AI retrieval and future model-training preparation require explicitly distinguished authority. Public graph/site outputs are filtered views of selected data, not automatically public mirrors of private collections.

## WHO
Ordinary people and collectors first; researchers, writers, artists and curators may publish; agents and external applications access only intentionally permitted material. Private/protected/scoped-agent/public are distinct. Optional password-unlocked protected content requires actual secure authentication and encrypted storage when confidentiality is promised.

## WHEN
**Personal:** save in seconds, annotate later, discover connections after years, transform old curation into publications in future. Preserve date and meaning across changing life periods.
**Technical:** first define data and negative security tests; then implemented catalog workflow; mixed media; AKF validators/round-trip; graph preview/attention; article publishing; optional AI intelligence. Implement incrementally and verify.
**Historical:** periodDate=2026-10-15; separate package revisions and future date-stamped amendments. Stage and production-value verification occur later. Historical documents are preserved, not rewritten.

## WHERE
Full-page Chrome extension and responsive side panel, already-authorized local companion storage, user-authorized R2 buckets, remote references, static-site output for generic hosting. Narrow side panels show an accessible alternative to split screen. Real private paths, access tokens and unapproved assets never belong in public website bundles.

## HOW
- Stable provider-independent logical item IDs; typed direct/indirect relationships with first-class explanations and provenance.
- Optional editable Title, descriptions, notes, dated notes and tags on all objects and edges.
- Permission-aware graph traversal, counts, filters, thumbnails, semantic search and export *before* serialization and aggregation.
- Separate canonical user meaning from presentation layouts, publication drafts, graphs, and dashboards.
- Maintain AKF schema version, fixtures, independent import/export, asset manifest and lossless migration.
- Explicit public / private / protected / scoped-agent access; separate training consent. A JSON field saying “private” cannot secure already-disclosed plaintext.
- Avoid technical jargon on user surfaces; optimize beauty, fluidity, accessibility, recoverability and low-memory performance.
- Test with real authorized R2/local providers, broken links, huge collections, unknown attention, revocation, and static output.

## Enduring product contract
**Beautiful for humans. Comprehensible to machines. Sovereign to its owner.** Time devoted to organization must become portable, reusable creative work, without sacrificing playful daily utility.
